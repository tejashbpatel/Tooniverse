Privacy Policy

Tooniverse: Comic Chat · Effective October 2026

What Tooniverse collects

The character name (handle) you choose, your cartoon character look, and anything you send or post: chats, doodles, posts, comic strips and photos. If you cartoonize a photo or take a selfie, the cartoon effect is made on your device, and only photos you choose or take are used. On supported iPhones you can also use Apple's Image Playground, which is provided by Apple under Apple's own terms.

Posts you share to the Toon Wall, and Daily Strips (which can include cartoon photos), are public. A Daily Strip disappears from the app after 24 hours. Your Collector's Vault and your cartoon face picture are kept on your phone only.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo for your character or a comic panel. The photo library is used only for photos you pick, and to save comic pages and stickers when you tap Save. We do not look through your library.

Where it is stored

Your profile, friend requests, squads, chats and posts are stored with Apple iCloud (CloudKit) in a shared online database. Because it is shared, other Tooniverse users may be able to see what you post or send. When you invite someone to a squad, your handle and the squad name are visible to the people you invite. Please don't share passwords, addresses, financial details or anything you want kept private.

What we don't do

We do not sell your information, show ads, or use third-party tracking or analytics. We never ask for your real name, phone number or address.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, they are used only to show alerts and badges for chats and friend requests, and one optional funny reminder each evening that you can turn off in Settings.

Your choices and deleting your account

You can block any character and report messages or posts. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Settings → Delete Account. Deleting removes your profile, friend requests, posts and messages we can find, and clears the app's data on your phone. You can also email us to ask for your data to be removed.

Children

Tooniverse is meant for people 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 is using the app, email us and we will remove the account.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page and in the app.

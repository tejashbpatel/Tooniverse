Support

We're happy to help!

Contact us

Email tejash.apps@gmail.com with your question, your character handle and, if you can, a screenshot. We read every message.

How do I turn a chat into a comic book?

Open any chat, tap MAKE COMIC, pick your pages, and save or share the book as a picture or PDF.

How do I create a squad?

Go to My Comic and open Ink Squads. Pick a cartoon world, name your squad and invite friends. They accept or decline from their Tooniverse home screen.

How do I report or block someone?

Open the chat and tap the menu at the top, then choose Report or Block Character. You can see and undo blocks in Settings → Blocked. You can also email us about anything that breaks the rules.

How do I delete my account?

  1. Open Settings in the app.
  2. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

This removes your profile, friend requests, posts and messages we can find, and clears the app's data on your phone.

Something isn't loading

  • Check you are signed in to iCloud (Settings → your name).
  • Check you have an internet connection.
  • Close and reopen the app.
